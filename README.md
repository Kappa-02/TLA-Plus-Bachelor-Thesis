# TLA+ for Software Correctness Verification

Bachelor’s thesis by **Federico Capitani**, **University of Perugia**, academic year **2024/2025**.

A technical study of TLA+ and its role in specifying and reasoning about the correctness of concurrent and distributed systems. The focus is on understanding and explaining existing formal methods, supporting tools, and published applications.

**[Read the thesis (PDF, Italian)](Tesi_361940_Federico_Capitani.pdf)**

## Topics covered

- Formal verification fundamentals: states, transitions, safety, and liveness properties.
- TLA+ specifications, temporal logic, and an introduction to PlusCal.
- Overview of the TLA+ Toolbox, TLC model checker, Apalache, and TLAPS.
- Specification and verification workflows, including incremental modelling and refinement.
- Case studies covering Two-Phase Commit and the published use of TLA+ at Amazon S3.
